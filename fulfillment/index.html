How it works

Four clear steps, from the first inbound shipment to a tracking number your customer can see.

Step 1

Send inventory

Ship your products to the hub. We receive each box, check it in, and get it ready for storage.

Step 2

We store

Inventory is organized in bins and kept ready to pick, so stock is easy to find when an order comes in.

Step 3

Orders ship

We pick, pack, and ship by the posted cutoff — same business day, or the next business day if an order arrives after cutoff.

Step 4

Track in ShipStation

See your stock and orders in ShipStation, so you always know what is on hand and what has already shipped.
